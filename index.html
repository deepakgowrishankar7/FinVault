🏃 Field Agent Passcode Help

Field Agent passcodes are private and assigned directly by the Admin (Financier).

To retrieve or reset your 4-digit agent passcode:
  1. Contact your Admin (Deepak Gowri Shankar).
  2. Admin will log into FinVault and open:
    Settings ➔ Team, Partners & Field Agents
  3. Admin can view your assigned passcode or edit it to a new 4-digit PIN.

📲 Use FinVault as Fullscreen App

To remove the browser top link bar and open FinVault with a clean, full-screen native mobile feel:

📱 On Android (Chrome, Samsung, Mi, Vivo)
  1. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) at the top right of your browser.
  2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Confirm by clicking "Install" or "Add".
  4. Open FinVault from your phone's home screen icon! The top link bar will be completely gone.
🍎 On iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share icon (bottom center).
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap Add at the top right.
